Rust XPBD Solver

Each grappler carries five 6-DOF trackers - hands, feet, and head. Mouse gizmos still work, or enter VR to drive either grappler with a headset, two controllers, and optional SteamVR foot trackers.

VR controls

Enter VR, choose a pose, turn the whole scene to line up with the controlled grappler, and position yourself before tracking. Point away from the menu and press either trigger once to snap on tracking; after releasing it, a trigger hold clutches that controller from the hand to the foot without snapping the foot.

For dedicated feet, run start-steamvr-demo.bat locally or start-steamvr-firebase.bat with this hosted page. When two SteamVR trackers are found they drive the feet automatically, while controllers stay on the hands. Allow the browser's Local Network Access prompt.

python -m pip install openvr cryptography
python scripts/steamvr_tracker_bridge.py

Position, player, distance, scene turn, floor height, stiffness, reset, and tracker-release controls are also available in the floating VR panel. Hold trigger on its blue corner and point around yourself to orbit the panel; point at a panel control and press any controller button to activate it.
