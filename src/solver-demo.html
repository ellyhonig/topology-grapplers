Rust XPBD Solver

Each grappler carries five 6-DOF trackers - hands, feet, and head. Mouse gizmos still work, or enter VR to drive either grappler with a headset, two controllers, and two SteamVR foot trackers.

VR controls

For local Windows use, start SteamVR and run start-steamvr-demo.bat. For the hosted page, download and start the tracker bridge on the SteamVR PC and allow the browser's Local Network Access prompt. Connect two trackers, enter VR, choose a pose, and position yourself with one tracker on each foot. Point away from the menu and press either controller trigger once to calibrate and start. The two lowest generic SteamVR devices are assigned left/right from their positions at that moment; controllers remain on the hands.

python -m pip install openvr
python scripts/steamvr_tracker_bridge.py --serve

Position, player, distance, floor height, stiffness, reset, and tracker-release controls are also available in the floating VR panel. Point at a panel control and press any controller button to activate it.
